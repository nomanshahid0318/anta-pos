🏗️ Fixed Assets Register

Durable, non-trading purchases only (shop decor, furniture, equipment, vehicles, POS hardware). Not for resale — stock for resale goes through Supplier GRN instead. Each asset depreciates straight-line over its useful life; the book value shown here automatically flows into the Balance Sheet, and the depreciation expense automatically flows into the P&L Statement.

📆 Prepaid / Deferred Expenses

Payments made now that cover future months — billboard/advertising paid upfront, prepaid rent, insurance, license fees, subscriptions, etc. The remaining (not-yet-amortized) balance shown here automatically flows into the Balance Sheet as an asset, and the monthly amortization automatically flows into the P&L Statement as an expense — one whole month at a time, evenly.

NameCategoryStoreStart DateTotal PaidMonthlyAmortizedRemainingStatusActions

🤲 Employee Advances / Loans

Cash advanced or loaned to an employee — this is NOT an expense, it's a receivable the company expects back (in cash or via salary deduction). The outstanding balance shown here automatically flows into the Balance Sheet as an asset. Only a formal Write Off turns the unpaid remainder into a real Bad Debt expense on the P&L.
